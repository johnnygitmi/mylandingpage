      League Descriptions

      Climb the ranks in our focus leagues! Each league represents a level of mastery in blocking distractions and staying productive.

      1. Byte Beginner League

      This is the starting point for all new users entering the world of focused productivity. Here, beginners learn to identify common distractions and add their first few sites to block. The league focuses on building basic habits like daily check-ins and simple goal setting.

      As members progress, they unlock tips on time management from retro computing eras. It's designed to be welcoming and low-pressure, encouraging consistent use of the tool.

      Many graduate quickly by maintaining a streak of focused sessions.

      "Start with one byte, and soon you'll code your destiny." - Ancient Terminal Sage

      2. Pixel Pioneer League

      Building on beginner skills, pioneers explore deeper into distraction management. They experiment with toggling blocks and analyzing their focus patterns. This league introduces community challenges inspired by old-school games.

      Members share pixel art representations of their productivity journeys. Advancement comes from blocking more complex sites like forums or news aggregators.

      It's where users start to see real improvements in their daily output. The emphasis is on exploration and fun in focus.
